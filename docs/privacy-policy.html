Privacy Policy – SquareCut Live

Last updated: 8 November 2026

SquareCut Live ("the app") is an Android app developed by Amanullah Khan, based in Saudi Arabia. It lets you livestream cricket matches to YouTube and Facebook at the same time, and optionally save the same feed to your phone. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. Summary

  • The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service.
  • We (the developer) do not receive, collect or store any of your data on our own servers.
  • Camera and microphone data is processed on your device. It is sent only to the streaming destinations you configure.
  • Your stream keys and settings are stored only on your device.

2. Data the app handles

  • Camera and microphone. Used only while you are live or recording, to build the video and audio feed. Live video and audio are sent to the YouTube and/or Facebook streaming servers that you choose. They are not sent to us.
  • Local recordings. If you choose a recording mode, MP4 files are saved to your phone's gallery in Movies/SquareCut Live. They stay on your device unless you share them yourself.
  • Stream settings. Your YouTube and Facebook server URLs and stream keys, the score ticker web address, video quality, recording mode, microphone mute state, and theme. These are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are sent only to the server addresses you entered.
  • Score ticker. The ticker page you enter is loaded inside the app and drawn over the video. Loading that page is a normal web request to the site you chose, and that site may collect data under its own policy.
  • Notifications. The app shows a notification while live or recording, with a Stop button.

3. Third parties

When you go live, your video and audio are delivered to YouTube (Google LLC) and/or Facebook (Meta Platforms, Inc.) according to the stream keys you provide. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies: Google and Meta. The score ticker page is provided by whichever website you choose.

4. Permissions

  • Camera, microphone: to capture the broadcast.
  • Internet, network state: to stream and to load the score ticker.
  • Notifications: to show the live or recording status.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the broadcast running when the screen is off.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save recordings to the public Movies folder.

5. Retention and deletion

Settings and stream keys remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Recordings remain in your gallery until you delete them. We hold no copies. To delete everything the app stores, uninstall it or clear its data in Android settings.

6. Security

Stream keys are stored in the app's private storage, which Android protects by sandboxing. They are not separately encrypted. Keep your device locked and do not share your stream keys.

7. Children

SquareCut Live is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
