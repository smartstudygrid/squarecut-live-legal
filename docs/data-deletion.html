Data Deletion – SquareCut Live

Last updated: 19 November 2026

SquareCut Live (com.squarecut.live) is an Android live-streaming app developed by Amanullah Khan. This page explains how to delete the data the app handles, including data received through Facebook Login.

1. What we store (and where)

We do not operate a server and we do not receive or keep copies of your data. Everything the app handles stays on your device:

  • Your settings, including YouTube/Facebook stream URLs and stream keys.
  • Your Facebook access token, name and the list of Pages you manage, when you sign in with Facebook.
  • Video recordings you choose to save to your gallery.

2. Delete everything in the app

  1. Open SquareCut Live → Settings → Facebook → Sign out. This discards the stored Facebook token and removes the app's access from your account on this device.
  2. Uninstall SquareCut Live, or open Android Settings → Apps → SquareCut Live → Storage → Clear data. This permanently deletes every setting, stream key and token the app stored.
  3. Delete any recordings the app saved: they are in your gallery under Movies/SquareCut Live.

If you recorded to a custom folder, delete those files the same way from your gallery or file manager.

3. Remove the app's Facebook access

Signing out in the app removes the connection on the device. To remove it from your Facebook account as well, and to revoke any permissions granted during login:

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Apps and Websites (on newer versions: Security and login → Apps and websites).
  3. Select SquareCut Live → Remove.

That revokes the app's access to your name, Page list and Page tokens. Meta's own record of the app's access is deleted under Meta's retention rules.

4. Delete data held by streaming platforms

Recordings of your broadcasts are held by the destination you streamed to, not by us. Use their tools to delete them:

  • YouTube Studio → Content, or your Facebook Page's video library / Facebook profile videos.
  • Their privacy policies apply to that data, not ours.

5. Request deletion by email

Because we hold no server-side copy of your data, there is normally nothing for us to delete. If you believe we hold data about you, or you want written confirmation, email amankhan@smartstudygrid.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the Facebook user ID or email you used. We will confirm within 30 days that no data is held or explain what, if anything, must be removed.
